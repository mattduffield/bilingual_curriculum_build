## Pin 1: sensory activity

**Title:** Puddle Play: An Easy Water Activity for 3-Year-Olds (Spanish and English)

**Description:** Here is a simple rainy-day sensory activity. Put 5 cm (2 inches) of water in a tub. Squeeze a sponge to make rain, pour between two cups, and talk about how it feels. The adult says "¡Una gota, dos gotas, un charco!" It uses things you already have, and an adult stays right next to the water. This activity is on a bilingual page with Spanish first and English beside it. Part of the Mi jardín / My Garden printable pack. [STORE LINK] #bilingualpreschool #sensoryplay #spanishforkids
