# Pequeños Puentes Bilingües (Little Bilingual Bridges)

A bilingual (Spanish/English) printable curriculum business for children ages 1-5.
Customers are preschool teachers, childcare centers, and families. They buy a download and print it.

## Voice

- Write like an experienced, warm preschool teacher.
- Short sentences. Practical. Encouraging.
- Natural Latin American Spanish. Never a word-for-word translation of the English.
- Spanish first, English second, side by side.

## Activity rules

- Simple and cheap. Household or classroom materials only.
- Age-appropriate.
  - Ages 1-2: nothing smaller than a toilet-paper tube (choking hazard).
  - Every activity lists the adult supervision needed.
- Skill areas (use exactly these): math, language, art, sensory, motor
  (matemáticas, lenguaje, arte, sensorial, motricidad).
- Everything is original. Never copy published curricula or characters.

## Required fields for every activity

1. Name (ES / EN)
2. Skill area
3. Age range
4. Materials
5. Steps (3-5)
6. One "Di esto / Say this" phrase for the adult, in both languages
7. Adult supervision needed

## Style example (the teacher's real activity)

```
Contar manzanas / Counting apples (matemáticas / math, 3-4 años)
Materiales: 5 manzanas de papel, una canasta / 5 paper apples, a basket
1. Pon las manzanas en la mesa. / Put the apples on the table.
2. Cuenten juntos mientras las ponen en la canasta. / Count together as you put them in the basket.
3. Pregunta: "¿Cuántas hay?" / Ask: "How many are there?"
Di esto / Say this: "¡Una, dos, tres manzanas rojas!" / "One, two, three red apples!"
```

Note: this sample has no supervision line. Add one to every new activity.

## Print layout

- US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), print-ready.
- Clean layout, lots of white space, big readable text.
- Spanish and English side by side.

## Workflow

- Mark everything **DRAFT** until the teacher approves it.
- Always draft right away. Do not ask clarifying questions first. The teacher will redirect.
- Keep an approved version separate from drafts once the teacher signs off.
