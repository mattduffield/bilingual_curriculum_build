# Review Checklist: Pequeños Puentes Bilingües

The teacher signs off on every pack before it is sold. Nothing leaves DRAFT until every box is checked.

**Pack:** ____________________  **Age:** ____  **Week:** ____  **Reviewer:** ____________________  **Date:** __________

Pack status values: `DRAFT` → `NEEDS REVIEW` → `APPROVED`.

## 1. Age-appropriateness and safety

- [ ] Every activity fits the age range printed on its page.
- [ ] Ages 1-2: no material smaller than a toilet-paper tube (choking hazard).
- [ ] Ages 3-5: any small or loose item (stones, buttons, beads) has a clear size rule.
- [ ] Nothing goes in mouths: paint, soil, dry food, small objects are all handled.
- [ ] Food-based materials (pasta, oats, flour, beans) have an allergy note.
- [ ] Every activity has a supervision line that says how close the adult must be.
- [ ] Supervision wording is consistent: "present the whole time" vs "nearby" mean the same thing on every page.
- [ ] Messy play says how to protect the table or floor and when to wash hands.
- [ ] Movement activities need clear floor space and nothing to trip on.
- [ ] Every material is a household or classroom item, cheap and easy to find.

## 2. Spanish correctness and natural phrasing

- [ ] No spelling, accent, or punctuation errors (¿ ? ¡ !).
- [ ] Natural Latin American Spanish, not a word-for-word translation.
- [ ] Same form of address throughout (tú to the adult, plural to the group). No "usted".
- [ ] Words work across Latin America, or the dialect choice is noted (e.g. café, cobija, tina).
- [ ] Wording includes girls and boys ("la niña o el niño", "cada niño").
- [ ] Short, warm sentences a teacher would really say out loud.
- [ ] The "Di esto" phrase is something an adult can say as written.
- [ ] The Spanish column contains only Spanish (unless a bilingual model phrase is chosen on purpose).

## 3. English correctness

- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors.
- [ ] It sounds natural, like a teacher talking. It is not a stiff copy of the Spanish.
- [ ] The English says the same thing as the Spanish (same numbers, same sizes, same steps).
- [ ] The English column contains only English (unless a bilingual model phrase is chosen on purpose).

## 4. Activity content

- [ ] Every activity has: name (ES/EN), skill area, age range, materials, 3-5 steps, "Say this" (ES/EN), supervision.
- [ ] Every material in the list is used in the steps, and every material used in the steps is listed.
- [ ] Every step works when you try it (numbers add up, questions have right answers).
- [ ] Learning objectives match what the activities really do.
- [ ] Vocabulary words (8-10) each appear in at least one activity.
- [ ] The week's materials list is consolidated: no duplicates, one item per line.

## 5. Originality

- [ ] All text was written fresh. Spot-check 3 phrases per activity with a web search.
- [ ] No published curriculum text, songs, rhymes, or activity sequences copied.
- [ ] No characters, brand names, or trademarks (in text, materials, or images).
- [ ] Rhymes and chants are original, not a known song with a few words changed.

## 6. Images we can legally sell

- [ ] Every image is drawn by us or licensed for commercial resale. Note the source: ____________________
- [ ] No clip art, stock images, or photos without a license that allows selling printables.
- [ ] Fonts are either system fonts or licensed for commercial use.
- [ ] No copyrighted or trademarked characters, even "inspired by".

## 7. Print layout

- [ ] Prints on US Letter at 100% with nothing cut off.
- [ ] Every page is exactly one printed page. No overflow, no blank pages.
- [ ] Text is big and readable. Lots of white space.
- [ ] Spanish on the left, English on the right, on every page.
- [ ] "DRAFT" shows on every page until approval (removed only after sign-off).
- [ ] The drawing box is big enough to use on every activity page.
- [ ] Vocabulary cards cut and fold cleanly.
- [ ] A test print was looked at on paper, not just on screen.

## Sign-off

- [ ] All sections above are complete.
- [ ] Teacher approves this pack for sale.
