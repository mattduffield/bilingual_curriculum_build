## What the brief asks for (to build live)

1. **Draft generator** by age (1–5), theme (e.g. *Las manzanas / Apples*), skill (math, language, art, sensory, motor) and language.
2. **Product pack:** cover and instructions, weekly plan with objectives, counting/vocabulary/art/motor activities, Spanish and English versions, letter-size PDF.
3. **Sales model:** digital product, automatic purchase and download; channels are Teachers Pay Teachers and Etsy, with Pinterest for promotion.

## The brief's six questions (answer each during the build)

1. How can AI turn my ideas into complete curricula without losing my style as a teacher?
2. What process creates, reviews and organizes many printables efficiently?
3. How can I automate publishing, promotion and sales?
4. What tools let a customer buy and download the file without me sending it?
5. How can I make sure the content is original, high quality and age-appropriate?
6. Could a system generate drafts by age, theme, skill and language?
