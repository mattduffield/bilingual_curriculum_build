# Pequeños Puentes Bilingües (Little Bilingual Bridges)

A bilingual (Spanish/English) printable curriculum business for children ages 1-5. Customers are preschool teachers, childcare centers, and families who buy a download and print it.

## Voice

- Write like an experienced, warm preschool teacher: short sentences, practical, encouraging.
- Spanish first, English second, side by side.
- Use natural Latin American Spanish. Never a word-for-word translation.

## Activity Rules

- Materials: household or classroom items only. Simple and cheap.
- Age-appropriate. For ages 1-2, nothing smaller than a toilet-paper tube (choking hazard).
- Every activity lists the adult supervision needed.
- Skill areas (use only these): math, language, art, sensory, motor.

## Required Activity Format

Every activity has:

1. Name (ES / EN)
2. Skill area (ES / EN)
3. Age range
4. Materials (ES / EN)
5. Steps (3-5, ES / EN)
6. Adult supervision needed
7. One "Di esto / Say this" phrase for the adult, in both languages

### Style example (match this)

```
Contar manzanas / Counting apples (matemáticas / math, 3-4 años)
Materiales: 5 manzanas de papel, una canasta / 5 paper apples, a basket
1. Pon las manzanas en la mesa. / Put the apples on the table.
2. Cuenten juntos mientras las ponen en la canasta. / Count together as you put them in the basket.
3. Pregunta: "¿Cuántas hay?" / Ask: "How many are there?"
Di esto / Say this: "¡Una, dos, tres manzanas rojas!" / "One, two, three red apples!"
```

## Layout

- US Letter size, print-ready.
- Clean layout, lots of white space, big readable text.

## Originality

- Never copy published curricula or characters. Everything is original.

## Workflow

- Mark everything **DRAFT** until the teacher approves it.
- Always draft right away. Don't ask clarifying questions first. The teacher will redirect.
