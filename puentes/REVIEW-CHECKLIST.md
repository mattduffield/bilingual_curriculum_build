# Review Checklist — Pequeños Puentes Bilingües

The teacher signs off on every item before a pack leaves DRAFT. Check the JSON and the printed pages.
If an item fails, write the page number and the fix in `review/week-N.md`.

## 1. Age-appropriateness and safety
- [ ] Every activity fits the age on the page (attention span, skills, directions).
- [ ] Ages 1-2: no item smaller than a toilet-paper tube.
- [ ] Ages 3-5: small items (beans, beads, buttons, pom-poms, toy parts) are flagged and a safer swap is offered.
- [ ] No small parts go in a mouth-play activity (sensory bins, water, dough).
- [ ] Food items in play: allergens named (oats/gluten, nuts, cocoa, flour, eggs) and a swap given.
- [ ] Paint, glue and markers are washable and non-toxic.
- [ ] Tools (forks, scissors, clothespins, tongs) are blunt or child-safe.
- [ ] Every activity has a supervision line, and it says how close the adult stays.
- [ ] Nothing needs a hot, sharp or electrical item.
- [ ] Materials are household or classroom items only, and cheap.

## 2. Spanish correctness and natural phrasing
- [ ] Spelling, accents and punctuation (¿ ? ¡ !) are correct.
- [ ] It sounds like natural Latin American Spanish, not a word-for-word translation.
- [ ] No word that is strange in a big region (e.g. balde / cubeta, frijoles / porotos). Use a common word or give two.
- [ ] Gender-neutral wording for children (la niña o el niño, los niños).
- [ ] The adult is addressed the same way throughout (tú or ustedes, not mixed).
- [ ] Short, warm sentences. Each step is one action.
- [ ] The "Di esto" phrase is something an adult would really say.

## 3. English correctness
- [ ] Spelling, grammar and punctuation are correct.
- [ ] It is not just a copy of the Spanish. It sounds natural to a US teacher.
- [ ] Short, warm sentences. Each step is one action.
- [ ] The "Say this" phrase is something an adult would really say.
- [ ] No Spanish in the English text and no English in the Spanish text, except the vocabulary.

## 4. Originality
- [ ] No text copied from a published curriculum, book, blog or website.
- [ ] No characters, songs, rhymes, logos or brand names that belong to someone else.
- [ ] No trademarked names in materials (use "play dough", not a brand).
- [ ] The business name and theme title have had a trademark search.
- [ ] Activities are written in our own words, even if the idea is common.

## 5. Images we have the right to sell
- [ ] Every image was drawn by us or is licensed for commercial resale. List each one and its source below.
- [ ] No clip art, stock photo or AI image without a written commercial license.
- [ ] No image looks like a known character or brand.
- [ ] Fonts are licensed for commercial use.

Image sources: ______________________________________________

## 6. Print layout
- [ ] US Letter. Every page prints on exactly one sheet (print preview, no blank pages).
- [ ] Nothing is cut off at the bottom or sides, with 0.5in margins.
- [ ] Text is big and readable. Plenty of white space.
- [ ] Spanish on the left, English on the right, on every page.
- [ ] "DRAFT" shows on every page until sign-off.
- [ ] Prints well in black and white.
- [ ] The word-card page cuts and folds cleanly.
- [ ] The draw box is big enough for a child.
